PASS if the default LinkedIn connection note variants are at most 200 characters (an extra variant labelled Premium may be up to 300; a character count shown is a plus), DMs are concise (roughly 100 words or less), and no message invents a connection, metric or experience not provided (e.g. no made-up user counts). FAIL if a default (non-Premium) connection note clearly exceeds 200 characters or a message fabricates facts.
